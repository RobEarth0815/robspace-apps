# 4Mail — Many Addresses, One Account

<img src="../images/icons/4mail.jpg" width="120" align="right" alt="4Mail app icon" />

**A mail app for people whose one mail account receives many addresses.**

Private, shop, club, support — all aliases of one account. 4Mail turns each address and each folder into a pigeonhole on your overview: a virtual mailbox with its own name, colour and rules that shows only what belongs to it. One account, but it feels like many separate mailboxes. Every reply goes out from the address the mail was sent to. No server in between: your mail travels only between your provider and your device.

*In review at the App Store.* · [Website](https://4mail.app.robspace.de)

---

## What it does

- **A mailbox for every address** — add an address as a pigeonhole and it shows the mail that was sent to that address, and only that. A coloured stamp on each mail shows which address it went to.
- **Pigeonholes from folders** — any folder, down to the smallest subfolder, can become a pigeonhole too. Each has its own name, colour, size and notifications.
- **Reply from the right address** — replies leave from the address the mail was sent to; you can still pick another sender.
- **Pigeonhole wall** — see at a glance where mail is waiting.
- **ToDos from mails** — one tap, with a due date and a snooze; optional automatic ToDos per pigeonhole.
- **Agenda** — appointments from your calendar and your ToDos, each on its day.
- **Briefing** — what is new, which replies are due, what is on today; summaries with Apple Intelligence **on your device**.
- **Search, safe reading, newsletter unsubscribe** — images and tracking pixels stay blocked until you load them; unsubscribing always asks you to confirm.
- **iPhone, iPad and Mac**; optional iCloud sync of your settings, addresses and ToDos (off by default, never mail content).

Providers do not tell apps about your aliases over IMAP, so you enter them once under Settings › Your addresses. To send as an alias, your provider must allow it.
