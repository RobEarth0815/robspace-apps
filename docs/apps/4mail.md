# 4Mail — All Addresses. One Glance.

<img src="../images/icons/4mail.jpg" width="120" align="right" alt="4Mail app icon" />

**A Gmail client for people who run several addresses through one mailbox.**

4Mail shows your inbox and your most important folders as a wall of pigeonholes — and every reply goes out from the address the mail came to. No server in between: your mail travels only between Gmail and your device.

*Coming soon — currently in beta (TestFlight).* · [Website](https://4mail.app.robspace.de)

---

## What it does

- **Pigeonhole wall** — Gmail labels, single addresses or saved searches as tiles with unread counts, colours and symbols.
- **Reply as the right address** — 4Mail recognises which of your addresses a mail was sent to.
- **ToDos from mails** — mark a mail as ToDo, set a due date, plan it for later; optional automatic ToDos per pigeonhole.
- **Agenda** — your calendars, dated ToDos and invitations in one timeline.
- **Briefing** — replies due, today's ToDos and appointments; summaries with Apple Intelligence **on your device**.
- **Search, notifications per pigeonhole, newsletter unsubscribe** (always with confirmation).
- **iPhone, iPad and Mac**; optional iCloud sync of your own settings and ToDos (off by default, never mail content).
