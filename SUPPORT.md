# Support

Need help with a robspace app? Here's where to go:

## Something is broken
→ [Open a Bug Report](https://github.com/RobEarth0815/robspace-apps/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml)

## You have an idea
→ [Open a Feature Request](https://github.com/RobEarth0815/robspace-apps/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml)

## You have a question
→ [Start a Discussion](https://github.com/RobEarth0815/robspace-apps/discussions)

## Anything sensitive (license, data privacy, business inquiry)
→ Email [support@robspace.de](mailto:support@robspace.de)

## You need it private and right now
→ Email [support@robspace.de](mailto:support@robspace.de). I'll do my best, but I'm one person — public issues usually get faster attention because other users can also chime in.

---

## Response time expectations

- **Critical bugs** (data loss, crash on launch, broken core feature): triaged same day if I'm awake, hotfix submitted to Apple as soon as TestFlight allows.
- **Normal bugs**: triaged within a few days, fix in the next release cycle.
- **Feature requests**: read promptly, answered with either "planned for vX.Y", "considering", or "no, because…". I don't leave them hanging.
- **Discussions**: I try to join in but they're community-driven — other users may answer faster than me.

---

## What's NOT here

- **App Store reviews** are handled inside the App Store itself, not here. If you've left a review and want me to respond, I will — through the App Store, which is the only channel where I can reply.
- **Source code** isn't public for the apps. This repo is community-only.
- **The web app for 4DI** lives at [4di.app.robspace.de/webapp](https://4di.app.robspace.de/webapp/) — same support channels apply.
